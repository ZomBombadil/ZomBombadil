# 🌌 ZomBombadil

Indie game dev & musician. I build dark worlds you can play and music you can get lost in.

## 🎮 Games

**TooDark** — 2D dark-fantasy action RPG. Real-time combat, infinite procedurally generated depths, gothic pixel art, torchlight and blood. Four classes, three companions, four story chapters, giant bosses every fourth depth. *In development.*

**Hatchem!** — Family-friendly egg-hatching monster collector. Hatch eggs, collect 21 original species across 5 rarities, level and evolve them, turn-based battles, TCG-style booster packs. Fully offline.
▶️ [Play in your browser](https://zombombadil.github.io/hatchem/) · 📦 [Repo](https://github.com/ZomBombadil/hatchem)

**ENTITY** — 3D third-person action RPG (Three.js). Four classes, five zones, flying mounts, WoW-style loot, recruitable AI sidekicks. *Parked — returning with a real art pipeline.*

## 🎵 Music

**Ainulindalë: Music of the Ainur** — a Silmarillion-themed album. Four tracks released, three to come.
